About GreenTech Solutions

Our Story: GreenTech Solutions started in 2023 when a small group of tech enthusiasts and environmental advocates noticed that small businesses wanted to go green but didn't know where to start. We decided to build simple, practical technology tools to bridge that gap.

Our Mission: To provide accessible, eco-friendly hardware and software solutions that help small and medium-sized businesses easily track, manage, and reduce their daily carbon emissions.

Our Milestones

2023 — The Idea

GreenTech Solutions was officially founded in a small home office. We launched our first basic prototype for a smart recycling bin monitor.

2024 — Going Commercial

We partnered with 50 local small businesses to test our energy-efficient workplace devices, helping them cut electricity waste by 20%.

2025 — Software Launch

We rolled out our EcoTrack Cloud software dashboard, making it simple for non-technical managers to view their business utility impacts.

2026 — Looking Ahead

Today, over 500 companies use our tools. We are actively developing new upgrades to keep making sustainable practices normal and affordable for everyone.
